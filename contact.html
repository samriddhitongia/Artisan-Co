Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, customisation and after-sales support.

How do I place an order?
Use our enquiry form to describe your project, quantities and timeline. Our team will respond within one business day with a formal quotation and specification sheet. For in-stock items, orders can be confirmed within 48 hours.
What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no minimum order quantity. We supply single pieces for individual executives as readily as we supply 500 workstations for a corporate campus. Pricing is unit-based for quantities under 10 and volume-discounted above that.
Can I customise dimensions, materials or finishes?
Yes — almost every product in our catalogue is customisable. We can adjust dimensions, specify alternative upholstery fabrics from our library of 200+ options, change leg finishes, and accommodate non-standard requirements. Custom pieces are made to order with lead times of 4–14 weeks depending on complexity.
Do you offer samples before ordering?
Yes. Fabric and material samples are available free of charge for projects above ₹5 lakh. For smaller orders, a sample box (including 5 fabric swatches and 3 finish chips) is available for ₹500 refundable against your first order. Visit our Palasia showroom to see full-size pieces in person.
What are your lead times?
In-stock items: 1–3 weeks. Made-to-order items: 4–14 weeks depending on the product and customisation level. Full lead times are listed on each product page. For urgent projects, speak to our team about expedited manufacturing — available on select product lines with a 15% surcharge.
Do you deliver across India?
Yes. We deliver pan-India with our own logistics team for orders within Madhya Pradesh and via trusted freight partners for other states. Delivery to metro cities typically takes 3–7 days from dispatch. For large project installations, we offer white-glove delivery with on-site assembly.
Do you handle installation?
Yes. Our installation team handles projects across central India. For out-of-state projects, we coordinate with certified installation partners in all major cities. Installation is included in project quotes above ₹10 lakh. For smaller orders, it is quoted separately.
What warranty do your products carry?
All structural frames carry a 5-year warranty. Upholstery and mechanisms are covered for 2 years. Gas lift cylinders (on task chairs) are warranted for 3 years or 100,000 cycles. Fabric and leather wear is not covered under warranty but we offer a repair and reupholstery service at preferential rates.
What is your return and cancellation policy?
In-stock items may be returned within 14 days of delivery in original condition for a full refund less return freight costs. Made-to-order and customised items cannot be returned unless they are defective. Cancellations on made-to-order pieces within 48 hours of confirmation are refunded in full; thereafter, a 30% cancellation charge applies to cover materials already committed.
Do you work with interior designers and architects?
Yes — the majority of our projects are specified by design professionals. We offer a trade programme with dedicated account management, priority sampling, and a 10% trade discount on first orders. Contact us to register your practice.
Can I visit your showroom?
Absolutely. Our Palasia showroom is open Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm and Saturday 10am–4pm. We recommend booking an appointment for project consultations so we can prepare relevant samples and have a senior consultant available. Walk-ins are always welcome.
Do you supply to government and public sector projects?
Yes. We are registered on GeM (Government e-Marketplace) and have experience with tendering processes for civic, municipal and government projects. Our cast iron lamp posts, bollards, railings and garden benches have been specified on multiple public infrastructure projects. We can supply GeM-compliant invoicing and certification.

Policies & Terms

Warranty Policy

  • 5 Years — All structural frames (steel, solid wood, aluminium)
  • 3 Years — Gas lift cylinders and recline mechanisms
  • 2 Years — Upholstery, fabric, leather and castors
  • 1 Year — Electrical components (sit-stand motors, power modules)

Warranty covers manufacturing defects and structural failure under normal commercial use. It does not cover damage from misuse, natural wear, or unauthorised modifications.

Delivery Policy

  • Indore & MP — In-house delivery team, 3–5 days from dispatch
  • Metro Cities — Freight partner network, 5–9 days from dispatch
  • Tier 2–3 Cities — 7–14 days from dispatch depending on location
  • Installation — Included on orders above ₹10 lakh; quoted separately below

All deliveries are insured. Damage in transit must be reported within 48 hours of receipt with photographic evidence for a claim to be processed.

Return & Cancellation Policy

  • In-Stock Items — 14-day return window, full refund less return freight
  • Made-to-Order — Non-returnable unless defective
  • Cancellation within 48h — Full refund, no questions
  • Cancellation after 48h — 30% charge applies on made-to-order items

To initiate a return or cancellation, email returns@artisanco.in with your order number. We process all valid returns within 7 business days of receiving the goods back.

Privacy Policy

  • Data Use — Enquiry details used solely to respond to your query
  • No Third-Party Sale — We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • Marketing — Opted-in subscribers only; unsubscribe anytime
  • Data Retention — Enquiry data retained for 3 years then deleted

This website does not use tracking cookies beyond basic analytics. Full privacy policy available on request. For data deletion requests, email privacy@artisanco.in.

Payment & Pricing Policy

  • Quotation Valid — 30 days from issue date
  • Payment Terms — 50% advance, 50% before dispatch (standard)
  • Large Projects — Milestone-based payment schedule available
  • GST — All prices exclusive of GST (18% applicable)

We accept NEFT, RTGS, cheque and major UPI platforms. Credit card payments attract a 2% processing surcharge. Purchase orders accepted from registered companies with approved credit terms.

Sustainability Policy

  • Wood Sourcing — FSC-certified or plantation timber only
  • Waste Reduction — Off-cut wood repurposed for smaller accessories
  • Packaging — Cardboard and paper-based packaging; no single-use foam
  • Local Supply Chain — 80% of materials sourced within India

We are working towards carbon-neutral operations by 2030. Our Pithampur facility runs on 60% solar power. Environmental specification sheets available for LEED and GRIHA project submissions.
