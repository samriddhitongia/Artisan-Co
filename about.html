Our Story

Two decades of bespoke craftsmanship from the heart of Indore.

Founded in Indore in 2005, Artisan & Co. began as a small workshop with a single principle: that the spaces where people work and live deserve furniture of lasting quality.

Two decades on, we supply bespoke furniture to some of India's most demanding clients — investment banks, five-star hotels, law firms, and technology campuses. Our manufacturing base in Pithampur employs 180 skilled craftsmen and women, many of whom have been with us since the beginning.

We are not a catalogue company. We are not a fast-furniture brand. We are a studio that happens to manufacture at scale, bringing the care of a bespoke atelier to projects of any size.
