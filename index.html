How to Choose the Right Chair

From executive boardrooms to open-plan task environments, the right chair supports performance and signals quality. Consider ergonomic range, upholstery durability rating, and base material before specifying.

Explore Chairs →
🎯

Match Chair to Use

Task chairs need lumbar adjustability and mesh backs for all-day use. Conference chairs prioritise aesthetics. Visitor chairs need compact footprint and durability.

📐

Measure Before You Order

Seat height should let feet rest flat. Armrests at desk height reduce shoulder strain. Allow 80cm clearance per workstation seat.

🧵

Choose the Right Upholstery

Mesh: breathable, ideal for warm climates. Leather: executive prestige, easy to clean. Fabric: 80,000+ Martindale rubs for commercial use.

⚡

Stack & Link for Training Rooms

Polypropylene shell chairs like our Bombay Training Chair stack 12-high and link in rows — essential for multi-use event spaces.

Choosing Sofas for Commercial Spaces

Lobby and lounge sofas must balance visual impact with durability. Look for Martindale rub ratings above 50,000, high-resilience foam, and frame warranties. Modular systems give layout flexibility.

Explore Sofas →
🏛️

Lobby vs Lounge

Lobbies need impressive silhouettes — low-profile with statement legs. Breakout lounges need deeper seats (60cm+) for relaxed posture.

🔧

Modular Advantage

Modular systems let you reconfigure layouts for events. Our Colaba Modular grows from 2-seat to 7-seat L-shape without buying new pieces.

🧼

Fabric for High Traffic

Performance velvet (100,000 Martindale) and leather-touch fabrics resist staining and abrasion in hotel lobbies and corporate receptions.

📏

Scale to Space

A 3-seater needs 2.5m clearance in front. Corner sofas anchor larger lobbies — pair with a coffee table at the same leg finish.

Selecting Tables & Desks

Conference tables set the tone of a boardroom. Executive desks signal authority. Coffee tables anchor seating clusters. Material and proportion are everything — solid wood for warmth, stone for permanence, glass for openness.

Explore Tables →
📐

Conference Table Sizing

Allow 60–75cm per person along the table length. A 10-person table needs at least 3m length. Don't forget legroom — aprons and central legs can obstruct seating.

🪵

Material Selection

Solid walnut: warm, executive. Marble: statement boardroom. Laminate: practical, durable for training rooms. Match table material to chair leg finishes.

🔌

Power & Data Integration

Conference tables should have in-table power modules. Specify power position before ordering — retrofitting is expensive and messy.

🔄

Folding Tables Save Space

Folding and nesting tables transform training rooms into event spaces in minutes. Our Venus Training Table nests flat for compact storage.

Outdoor & Garden Furniture Guide

Outdoor furniture must withstand UV, rain, dust and heat cycles. Specify powder-coated aluminium or Grade A teak. Stainless steel hardware throughout. UV-stable fabrics above 1000 hours.

Explore Outdoor →
☀️

UV & Weather Resistance

Powder-coated aluminium won't rust and weighs less than steel. Grade A teak has natural oils that resist warping even in monsoon conditions.

🌧️

Fabric Matters Outdoors

Solution-dyed acrylic fabrics (Sunbrella-grade) retain colour for 5+ years outdoors. Avoid polyester blends that fade and hold moisture.

⚓

Anchor Heavy Pieces

Bollards, lamp posts and benches in public spaces should be anchor-bolted. We supply fixing plates and specification drawings for civic projects.

🔒

Security for Public Spaces

Specify anti-vandal fixings and through-bolted frames for parks and civic plazas. Our cast iron benches include tamper-proof SS hardware.

Lounge & Accent Chair Selection

Lounge chairs are the punctuation of interior design — they create conversation clusters, define zones, and add character. Prioritise silhouette first, then upholstery, then scale to room height.

Explore Lounge Chairs →
🎭

Create Zones with Chairs

Two facing lounge chairs with a coffee table creates an intimate conversation zone in any lobby. Use contrasting fabric to define the cluster.

🎨

Colour as Wayfinding

In large open-plan offices and hotels, coloured lounge chairs serve as visual anchors — guests naturally gravitate to distinctive seating clusters.

🔊

Wing Chairs for Acoustic Privacy

High-back wing chairs reduce ambient noise for the occupant by creating a partial acoustic enclosure — ideal for hotel lobbies and co-working spaces.

📦

Ottoman Pairings

Always specify an ottoman at the same time as your lounge chair — matching frame finish and fabric ensures a coordinated look and avoids stock mismatches.

Healthcare Furniture Specification

Hospital furniture must meet hygiene, durability and accessibility standards. Specify anti-bacterial upholstery, wipe-clean surfaces, and chairs with arm supports to assist patient transfer. BIFMA and EN certifications matter.

Explore Hospital Furniture →
🧪

Anti-Bacterial Fabrics

Specify vinyl or anti-bacterial performance fabric for patient areas. Our hospital range uses fabrics tested to EN 13795 for healthcare environments.

♿

Accessibility First

Seat heights of 46–48cm assist patient transfer. Arm supports on both sides are essential. Avoid castors in patient rooms — use glides or fixed feet.

🔧

Easy Maintenance

Choose wipe-clean surfaces for bedside cabinets and overbed tables. Concealed fixings prevent dirt traps. All our hospital pieces have tool-free height adjustment.

🏥

Zone by Function

Waiting areas need durable linked seating. Patient rooms need bedside cabinets and overbed tables. Staff areas need ergonomic task chairs rated for shift work.

Café & Restaurant Furniture Guide

F&B furniture gets daily punishment — spills, scratches, constant use. Stackability saves storage space. Specify commercial-grade frames, wipe-clean surfaces and 200,000+ cycle tested joints for any busy food service operation.

Explore Café Range →
🪑

Stackable = Practical

Stackable chairs cut storage footprint by 80% and allow quick layout changes between breakfast, lunch and dinner service. Our café range stacks 8-high.

🍽️

Table Height Standards

Standard dining: 75cm. Bar/counter: 105cm. Specify table heights before chairs — mismatched heights are a common and costly mistake in restaurant fitouts.

🔒

Booth Seating for Intimacy

Fixed booth seating maximises covers per square metre and creates acoustic privacy between tables — ideal for restaurants targeting a premium dining experience.

🌦️

Al Fresco Considerations

Terrace furniture must handle rain and sun. Our Terrace Café Set uses UV-powder-coated aluminium frames and PE rattan weave that won't fade or crack.
